# PocketSmart-AI – Ideation Phase

## Project Title
PocketSmart-AI

## Project Idea
PocketSmart-AI is an AI-powered smart budget planner and recommendation assistant.

## Problem Statement
Managing daily expenses and planning a budget can be difficult. Users need a simple way to track their expenses and receive useful budget recommendations.

## Proposed Solution
PocketSmart-AI helps users track their daily expenses and provides AI-based budget recommendations and smart saving tips using Google Gemini.

## Main Objectives
- Track daily expenses
- Help users plan their budget
- Provide AI-based budget recommendations
- Give smart saving tips
- Make personal budget management simple and easy

## Technologies Planned
- Python
- Google Gemini AI
- Streamlit

## Expected Outcome
A simple AI-powered budget assistant that helps users understand their spending and make better budgeting decisions.
